Privacy policy

Candy Warfare: Hell March for Android · by Two Imps Games · last updated 2026-11-12

In short: the game has no accounts and doesn't send your progress anywhere: it stays on your phone. Optional reward videos come from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show them. Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

Who we are

Candy Warfare: Hell March is made by Two Imps Games. You can reach us at twoimpsgames@gmail.com.

What the game keeps on your phone

Your progress (waves, candies on your board, gems, pets, upgrades, story scenes seen), your settings and a record of what you've bought are saved on your phone, inside the app. They aren't sent to us. We don't run a server, we don't ask for your name or email, and the game has no chat or account.

You can erase this data at any time with Menu › Reset progress, or by uninstalling the game. If your phone's backup is turned on, Android may include it in your own Google backup, which you control in your phone's settings.

Reward videos (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional reward videos, for example to double the sugar your Candy Factory made while you were away. A video only plays when you tap a button that says what you'll get. The videos come from Google AdMob. To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location;
  • how you interact with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps and video views);
  • diagnostic information about the app's and the SDK's performance;
  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice any time under Menu › Privacy choices. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on the phone). Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general and parental-guidance audiences.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Players with No Ads get the rewards without watching videos.

Purchases (Google Play)

Gem packs, the Starter Pack and No Ads are bought through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment and your payment details; the game only receives the purchase itself (which product, and Google Play's order number), so it can hand it over and give it back if you reinstall. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. The game has no subscriptions.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone. If you're a parent and have a question, write to us.

Selling or sharing data

We don't sell personal information, and we don't receive the data the ad SDK collects: it goes to Google, as described above, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google may use it to show personalised ads, which some US state laws call "sharing"; you can turn off personalised ads in your phone's settings, as described above.

Changes

If the game starts doing something new with data, this page will say so before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
