Candy Warfare: Hell March

An idle merge game for Android, by Two Imps Games

Lucifudge and his demons smashed the gates of the Candy Castle and stole half the Sugarheart's light. Summon candy heroes, merge them into stronger ones and march them all the way down into hell to bring the light home.
